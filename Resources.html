library/books

Essential Books

Three titles you'll find on every Linux bookshelf — pick the one that matches where you are.

Beginner

The Linux Command Line

by William Shotts

The classic first book: 500+ pages that take you from your very first keystroke to writing your own shell scripts. Free to read online.

Read free online
Internals

How Linux Works

by Brian Ward

What actually happens between the power button and the login prompt — boot, processes, devices and networking, demystified.

View book
SysAdmin

UNIX & Linux System Administration Handbook

by Evi Nemeth et al.

The administration bible used in universities and data centers for decades — servers, storage, security and more.

Official site
